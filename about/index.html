About

What CAT is, and who it is for.

What CAT is

Cambridge Alignment Theory (CAT) is a theoretical AI safety organisation based in Cambridge. Our interest is the theory: the mathematical questions underneath the problem of making AI systems safe.

Who it is for

Mathematicians and physicists who are curious about AI safety and want to know where to start. Our first talk is an overview of the field, with pathways for learning more.

Where we are now

We have just started. The first talk is given by Mikhail Moronov on Friday 27 November 2026 at 5:30pm, in MR2 at the Centre for Mathematical Sciences.

Our first program, WHISKERS, is a two-week intensive at Meridian from 7 to 19 December. Details are on the Programs page.
