Winter Hub for Intensive Study of Key Elements in Rigorous Safety.

Two weeks at Meridian, Cambridge, from 7 to 19 December 2026.

Two-week intensive

Meridian, Cambridge

Two meals a day, unlimited snacks

What it is

An in-person introduction to theoretical alignment research, for established students and researchers in mathematics and physics.

WHISKERS follows the lectures of MISO, a two-week school on the mathematics of AI safety run by MAISI and Iliad in Berkeley. We watch the lectures online together, work through problem sets on the material, and discuss it among ourselves.

Two meals a day are provided, and snacks are unlimited.

If you cannot attend the programme in-person but would like to attend remotely, or if you require funding to attend the programme, please contact us at cat.alignment@gmail.com

Lectures run Monday to Saturday. Lectures run 60 to 75 minutes including questions; the rest of each slot is a break. Times may shift a little. All lectures will be watched together in-person at Meridian.

Schedule

Lectures run Monday to Saturday, following the usual Cambridge week, with Sundays free. Each day has a theme and four lectures from MISO, listed with their speakers, and afternoons end with problem sets and discussion. The last few days are still to be decided.
