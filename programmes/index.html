WHISKERS

Winter Hub for Intensive Study of Key Elements in Rigorous Safety

Two weeks introducing mathematicians and physicists to theoretical alignment research. We watch recorded lectures from MISO together and discuss them. MISO is a two-week conference on the mathematics of AI safety, run by MAISI and Iliad in Berkeley, where leading researchers from theoretical AI safety labs present their frontier research to fellow mathematicians.
