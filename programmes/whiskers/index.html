WHISKERS

Winter Hub for Intensive Study of Key Elements in Rigorous Safety.

Two weeks in Cambridge, from 7 to 18 December 2026.

Two-week intensive

Monday 7 to Friday 18 December 2026

Cambridge

Two meals a day, plus snacks

What is it?

An in-person introduction to theoretical alignment research, for students and researchers in mathematics and physics.

Format: We watch the recorded lectures of MISO together in Cambridge and discuss them. MISO is a two-week conference on the mathematics of AI safety, run by MAISI and Iliad in Berkeley, where leading researchers from theoretical AI safety labs present their frontier research to fellow mathematicians. The MISO organisers have kindly allowed us to use their recordings.

Who it’s for: The programme aims to prepare lecturers for a Lent term lecture series on theoretical AI safety for mathematicians at the University of Cambridge. We therefore expect the median participant to be a PhD student, but we encourage anyone interested to apply, from undergrad to faculty!

Need funding to attend? If you need financial support to take part, for example for travel or accommodation, we can’t offer grants ourselves, but we can endorse your application to BlueDot’s Rapid Grants, which may improve your chances of being funded.

Applications: WHISKERS is application only. Questions go to cat.alignment@gmail.com.
