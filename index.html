Cambridge Alignment Theory

The CAT (Cambridge Alignment Theory) Research Group is a Cambridge-based AI safety fieldbuilding organisation, focused on engaging mathematicians in theoretical research. In our inaugural term this Michaelmas, we will be hosting multiple talks introducing mathematicians to the field of theoretical AI safety. We'll also be hosting an intensive programme running 7–19 December, aimed at introducing established students and researchers to the field of theoretical alignment research, with a view to extend this to a regular lecture series in Lent.

Mikhail Moronov

An overview of the field of theoretical AI safety, with pathways for mathematicians and physicists who want to learn more.
