Cambridge Alignment Theory

The CAT (Cambridge Alignment Theory) Research Group is a Cambridge-based AI safety fieldbuilding organisation, focused on engaging mathematicians in theoretical research. In our inaugural term this Michaelmas, we will be hosting multiple talks introducing mathematicians to the field of theoretical AI safety. We'll also be hosting an intensive program running Dec. 7 - 19, aimed at introducing established students and researchers to the field of theoretical alignment research, with a view to extend this to a regular lecture series in Lent.
