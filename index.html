Cambridge Alignment Theory

Cambridge Alignment Theory is a Cambridge-based AI safety fieldbuilding organisation, focused on engaging mathematicians in theoretical research. In our inaugural term this Michaelmas, we will be hosting multiple talks introducing mathematicians to the field of theoretical AI safety.

We'll also be hosting an intensive programme running 7–18 December, aimed at introducing established students and researchers to the field of theoretical alignment research, with a view to extend this to a regular lecture series in Lent.

First talk of term

Friday 27 November 2026
5:30pm

MR2, Centre for Mathematical Sciences

An introduction to theoretical AI safety

Mikhail Mironov

I’ll give an overview of theoretical AI safety, its theory of change and where it fits in the wider AI safety picture, as well as the main research agendas and organisations working on it. I’ll also explain how to get involved, with different routes for undergraduates and master’s students, PhD students and postdocs, and faculty. I won’t be proving any maths statements, so no background is needed.
