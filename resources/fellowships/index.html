Fellowships and funding

Last updated: November 2026.

Types of AI safety research

This is a rough categorisation. In practice, the types often overlap, especially the last two.

  • Empirical safety research is mostly machine learning research – running lots of experiments or building benchmarks.
  • Theoretical safety research is mostly maths research.
  • Conceptual safety research is mostly philosophy.

Theoretical AI safety: fellowships

ShowHide ↓

Fellowships

  • AFFINE seminar (1 month), then the AFFINE fellowship (1 year)
  • AI Safety Camp (3 months, unpaid): sometimes has agent foundations mentors
  • Iliad fellowship (3 months, after the 1-month Iliad Intensive), then a 6-month extension.
  • MATS theory track (3 months), then a 6–12 month extension
  • PIBBSS fellowship (3 months): some mentors do theoretical research
  • Many labs, such as ARC, AIXI Labs and Dovetail, sometimes take fellows or interns, which can lead to jobs

Postdoc-style positions (1+ years)

  • CHAI fellowship
  • Fields Institute postdoc (Mathematics for AI Safety)

Empirical AI safety: programmes and fellowships

ShowHide ↓

Based on this roadmap.

1. ML and research upskilling

  • ARENA (1 month): in-person bootcamp, or the online material; the default option
  • ARBOx (2 weeks): an ARENA-based bootcamp in Oxford.
  • ML4Good (7 days): in-person bootcamp, more entry-level than ARENA
  • Apart hackathons (3 days)
  • BlueDot Technical AI Safety Project Sprint (25 hours across 5 weeks)

2. Part-time, unpaid, online fellowships (each ~3 months)

  • SPAR: the default entry point
  • AI Safety Camp
  • Algoverse AI Safety
  • Apart Fellowship
  • MARS (run by CAISH; first week in person)

3. Full-time, paid, on-site fellowships (each ~3 months)

  • Astra
  • CBAI
  • CHAI
  • ERA: local, in Cambridge
  • LASR
  • MATS
  • Pivotal
  • Anthropic Fellows (4 months)
  • OpenAI Safety Fellowship (5 months)

  • MATS theory track (3 months), then a 6–12 month extension

Funding

ShowHide ↓
  • BlueDot Rapid Grants: up to $20k, for example compute for a project.
  • BlueDot Career Transition Grants: $20k–$200k to move full-time into AI safety.
  • BlueDot Seed Grants: $20k–$200k for the first 6–12 months of a new project or organisation.
  • Project Tailwind: Coefficient Giving’s programme to fund founders starting new AI safety organisations.

Mikhail Mironov, one of CAT’s advisors, works at Meridian, which is in contact with BlueDot. We can endorse your BlueDot grant application, which may improve your chances.
