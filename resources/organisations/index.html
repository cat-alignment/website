Theoretical AI safety organisations

We are trying to keep this list exhaustive. If we have missed an organisation, please let us know.

All numbers on this page are our rough estimates of long-term full-time staff, not fellows, interns or visiting researchers.

Theoretical AI safety is a small field. We estimate that 100–150 people work on it full-time. The table above leaves out academics and independent researchers, so its team sizes add up to less than 100–150. For comparison, about 600–800 researchers work full-time on empirical AI safety (extrapolated to 2026 from this 2025 analysis).
