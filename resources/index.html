Background and prerequisites

Good starting context and reading, and some resources for plugging gaps. All have a very low barrier to entry.

  • Iliad prerequisitesAdapted from ARENA’s list, with a more theoretical focus.
  • ARENA prerequisitesAimed more at empirical work. Good starting reading, and an idea of the most applicable areas of mathematics.

Introductory papers
